# FlowMate AI

## Short description

Manage email, calendar, work, and study with Bob, your AI assistant.

## Full description

FlowMate AI brings your daily work together in one application.

- Keep track of personal schedules and checklists.
- Connect Gmail and Google Calendar only when you choose to grant access.
- Summarize email, draft replies, and detect meeting suggestions.
- Chat with Bob to research, plan, and work through tasks.
- Manage projects, tasks, and status reports in Business workspaces.
- Keep your mobile and web experience synchronized.

You stay in control of your Google connection, shared content, and account
data. Sensitive actions require clear confirmation before they run. You can
permanently delete your account and personal data from Settings.
